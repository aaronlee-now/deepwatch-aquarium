# Room image prompts

Security-camera style prompts for each room image. Doorways are listed left → center → right.

## Global rules

- **No live animals.** Do not show fish, sharks, jellyfish, turtles, rays, penguins, or any other sea creatures anywhere in the scene — not in tanks, not through doorways, not in exhibits. The game adds moving threats separately; static animals confuse players and look wrong when they don't move.
- **Plush toys / merchandise** in the gift shop are OK (they are products, not threats).
- **Decorative silhouettes** (flat wall art, logos) are OK — but not realistic animals.

Use the same closing line on every prompt:

> WET REFLECTIVE FLOORING, DARK BLUE-GRAY LIGHTING, ATMOSPHERIC AQUARIUM DESIGN, REALISTIC ENVIRONMENT, NO PEOPLE, NO LIVE ANIMALS, NO FISH, NO SEA CREATURES ANYWHERE IN THE SCENE, EMPTY TANKS AND EXHIBITS WITH CORAL AND PLANTS ONLY, NO UI, NO HUD, NO CAMERA OVERLAY.
