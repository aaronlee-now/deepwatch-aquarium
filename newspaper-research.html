FNaF Newspaper Research

What the real Five Nights at Freddy’s newspapers do — and how Deepwatch Aquarium copies the idea

1. When it shows up

In Five Nights at Freddy’s 1, clicking New Game shows a newspaper for a few seconds. Then the first night starts (often with a 12 AM clock).

New Game → HELP WANTED newspaper → 12 AM → Night 1

Deepwatch uses the same order: newspaper → 12 AM → Night 1.

2. The HELP WANTED ad (exact idea)

The main thing is not a long news story. It is a job ad in a thick black box. Fans copied the wording carefully. Here is the FNaF 1 version:

HELP WANTED

Freddy Fazbear’s Pizza

Family pizzeria looking for security guard to work the nightshift.

12 am to 6am.

Monitor cameras, ensure safety of equipment and animatronic characters.

Not responsible for injury/dismemberment.

$120 a week.

To apply, call:

1-888-FAZ-FAZBEAR

Later game updates changed the phone number in some versions (for example to a 1-555 number). The job text stayed the same idea.

3. How the page looks (style research)

The Help Wanted screen is meant to feel like a close-up photo of a newspaper clipping, not a clean website.

Font

Courier (typewriter look). Fans and font guides match this to the clippings.

Colors

Mostly grayscale / gray newsprint — not bright yellow paper.

Camera angle

Zoomed in and slightly tilted / crooked on a black background.

Background text

Busy columns around the ad, blurred so you cannot read them easily.

4. The blurry “blah blah” filler

Around the ad, the same paragraph is repeated many times (about nine times on the FNaF 1 page). Fans un-blurred it. It is a joke from the creator, Scott Cawthon — not secret lore.

Blah. Blah. Blah, Blah. Blah. This ad has nothing to do with anything relevant to the game. Blah. Blah. Blah. Blah. Blah. Chances are you won’t make it past Night 3. Blah. Blah. Yackity Smackity. Blah. Blah. This probably isn’t the best choice of a summer job, since you most likely won’t survive the week. I’d recommend being a cashier, sack boy, or work in a warehouse. They are all very respectable jobs, and you probably won’t be killed by them. Well, you might. But it would be unlikely. Blah. Blah.

5. Other newspapers in FNaF 1

Later nights can show other clippings about the restaurant’s past. Those are different from the New Game Help Wanted ad. For a kid-friendly aquarium game, we copy the Help Wanted intro, not the spooky later articles.

6. Why this design works for a game

  • It feels like a real job posting, so the story starts fast.
  • The hours 12 AM to 6 AM teach the win goal.
  • “Monitor cameras” tells you the main gameplay.
  • The silly filler text makes the page look busy like a real paper.
  • A short wait, then the night begins — no long movie needed.

7. Deepwatch Aquarium version

The browser game (index.html + game.js) is the main version. It copies the FNaF recipe:

  • HELP WANTED box in the center
  • Deepwatch Aquarium night-guard job
  • 12 am to 6am + monitor cameras
  • $120 a week and phone: 1-888-DEEP-FISH
  • Kid-friendly warning (soggy shoes / runaway crabs)
  • Courier font + gray newsprint + tilt
  • Blurred “blah” columns around the ad
  • Auto-continues after a few seconds (or click)

HELP WANTED

Deepwatch Aquarium

Family aquarium looking for security guard to work the nightshift.

12 am to 6am.

Monitor cameras, ensure safety of equipment and sea exhibits.

Not responsible for soggy shoes or runaway crabs.

$120 a week.

To apply, call:

1-888-DEEP-FISH

Open the browser game, click New Game, and you will see that newspaper before Night 1.

The old Python / pygame file (game.py) is deprecated — keep using the browser version for new features.
